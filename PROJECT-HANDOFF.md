# Housewatch project handoff

Housewatch is a rental search platform for Delft. It brings listings from several sources into one place so people can compare homes, keep a shortlist, and follow up through the original advertisement. The project grew out of a student housing search near TU Delft.

## What Housewatch does

- **Collects listings.** Housewatch checks rental sites and local agents for rooms, studios, and apartments. It keeps the original listing link and available details, tracks changes in price and availability, and links duplicate offers where it can. Connected sources can also include saved-search emails and authorized housing groups.
- **Checks listings against a search.** People can set their budget, move-in dates, and minimum stay. Housewatch reads the advertised terms, including rent and bills, tenancy length, registration, and applicant restrictions. Missing or conflicting information is flagged for review.
- **Estimates travel times.** When a listing has a usable location, Housewatch calculates walking, cycling, and, where timetable data is available, public transport estimates to TU Delft Aula and nearby amenities such as a supermarket and a gym. Estimates based on a street or postcode are marked as approximate.
- **Helps people keep track.** People can save or pass on listings and keep private notes. Optional alerts help them catch new matches.

## What to keep in mind

Coverage varies by source. Some listings omit an exact address or leave costs and availability unclear. Housewatch helps people spot what they still need to confirm with the original source. Saving or passing on a listing is private. Any enquiry, application, or booking is handled by the person searching.
